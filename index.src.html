What the extension tells you. At a glance.

Lists each Pokémon’s top five moves

From Pikalytics, for your format. The small figure is how often players run each move.

Turns red when a move can KO your Pokémon

Damage as a share of your Pokémon’s HP, lowest roll to highest. Red means the top roll knocks it out.

Counts each Pokémon’s stat changes

Intimidate’s −1 Attack or a Swords Dance’s +2: each stage on the field goes into the numbers.

Uses your Pokémon’s Pokepaste stats

Paste a Pokepaste link into the toolbar popup and damage uses your natures, EVs and IVs.

Where the numbers come from

Move usage comes from Pikalytics, HP and stat stages from the battle, and damage from the engine behind Showdown’s own calculator. Where the panel cannot know something, like the opponent’s Attack, it assumes the worst case.

Install it in 3 steps

It is not on the Chrome Web Store yet, so you build it from the code.

  1. Install Node.js and clone the repository, then run npm install and npm run build.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and pick the folder.
  3. Start or watch a battle on Pokémon Showdown. The panel docks on the right.

Questions

Each answer is what the extension does today.

Which opposing Pokémon does it show?

The ones on the field now, one card each: two in doubles. It updates once each move’s animation ends.

Where does the extension get its move data?

From Pikalytics, for the format you are playing. If it has nothing for that format, the panel uses the nearest format it has and says so.

How does the extension work out damage?

With @smogon/calc, the engine behind Showdown’s calculator. It gives the opponent maximum Attack and no item or Tera type until Showdown shows them.

Does it work in Pokémon Champions?

Yes. A Pokémon that can Mega Evolve gets a button for each Mega form; press one and its moves and damage follow that form.
